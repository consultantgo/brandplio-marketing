01 · Extract

From a URL to a brand system. Two ways.

Quick crawls your public site automatically and proposes a full brand system in minutes. Advanced puts you in control: pick the exact pages that shape your voice, flag products inline, and see afterward which pages actually mattered.

  • Sitemap-first discovery. We pull sitemap.xml (and the index, gzipped variants, robots.txt directives), then fall back to a smart crawl if there’s nothing to fetch.
  • Curated review with smart defaults. Homepage, about, top products, recent blog posts, pricing, contact: pre-checked. Override anything with one click, up to 40 pages.
  • Per-URL roles. Voice, product, or excluded: every page declares what it shapes before extraction even runs.
  • Page-level attribution after the run. See exactly which URLs shaped brand voice, which were classified as products, and which were excluded. Auditable, not a black box.
  • Fast and low-cost. Most runs finish in around two minutes.

04 · Detect

Off-brand combinations, caught at compose time.

When you pick a persona, a product, and a tone that shouldn’t mix (say, an urgent tone for a risk-averse audience, or a celebratory frame for a sensitive announcement), Brandplio flags the tension before a single word of copy is generated.

  • Rules + learned signals. Hand-authored guardrails plus patterns inferred from your own flagged content.
  • Severity-aware. warn, block, or suppress: your call per combination.
  • Also surfaced at the API. Tensions come back in the response, so downstream agents can react.

06 · Govern

One queue. Every flag. Every brand.

Content flagged by the analyzer, tensions raised at compose time, restricted claims caught by the vocabulary engine: all of it lands in a single review queue. Approve, edit, or reject. Every action is tracked.

  • Role-based routing. Send each flag to the right person: brand, content, or reviewer.
  • Change history. Every approve, reject, and edit is attributed and timestamped.
  • Closes the loop. Reject with a reason, and the rule that caught it gets a learning signal.
